# Peashooter

<p align="center"><img src="../../images/characters/peashooter.png" alt="Peashooter" width="320"></p>

**Plant · shooter** · Plants & Zombies (original)

The basic shooter: fires peas at zombies it can see. Cheap, and the starting point for several stronger plants.

## Abilities

- Shoots zombies of any kind; vanilla zombies, husks, drowned and zombified piglins count too. A tamed Peashooter also shoots other monsters, but never creepers.
- Aims ahead of moving targets.
- Shoots back at whoever hurts it, and nearby Peashooters join in. A tamed one also attacks what its owner attacks and whatever attacks its owner.
- A pea that flies through fire turns into a fire pea and sets the target alight.
- While a tamed Peashooter grows seeds on farmland, it does not shoot.

## Interactions

- Struck by lightning, it turns into an [Electric Peashooter](electric_peashooter.md).
- Seeds grown on farmland can mutate: during a thunderstorm 50% become an [Electric Peashooter](electric_peashooter.md), in a crimson forest 50% a [Fire Peashooter](fire_peashooter.md), otherwise 10% a [Repeater](repeater.md).
- A wild one is tamed by feeding it sun: about 36% chance per sun.
- Relatives with other peas: [Snow Pea](ice_peashooter.md), [Repeater](repeater.md).
