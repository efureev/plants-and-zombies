# Bonk Choy

**Plant · melee fighter** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A close-combat plant that punches anything within reach and now and then knocks it into the air with an uppercut.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:bonkchoy` |
| Health | 20 |
| Sun cost | 4 |
| Size | 0.6 × 0.8 blocks |
| Attack | punch about every 1.15 s, 4 damage; uppercut about every 2.9 s, 8 damage |
| Range | notices targets within 4 blocks |
| At night | stays awake |
| How to get | raid rewards in the Mailbox (regular waves, first 8 rewards); grows wild in savannas and the Bamboo Jungle; a wild one is tamed with sun (~45% per sun) |

## Abilities

- After every attack it picks the next one at random: 45% uppercut, otherwise a plain punch.
- The uppercut does double damage and throws the target up and away.
- Punches have a light knockback.
- Targets zombies (mod and vanilla); a tamed one also fights other hostile mobs, except creepers.
- A wild Bonk Choy also punches players who come close.

## Interactions

- Can wear a [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md).
