# Browncoat

**Zombie · basic** · Plants & Zombies (original)

The most common zombie of the mod and the backbone of every raid. On its own it is as weak as a vanilla zombie, but it often wears a cone, a bucket or a door, may float on balloons and changes its look with the biome.

## Abilities

- Fights like a vanilla zombie: melee hits, breaks doors, may get random vanilla armour and weapons and pick up items.
- Mod equipment, with a chance that grows with regional difficulty (and with raid progress): a Cone or a bucket on the head, a Door Shield in the off hand (blocks hits from the front), a Coffin on the chest. Spawn eggs with these items are named Conehead, Buckethead, Screen Door, Coffin and Fully Armored Zombie.
- 2 % of naturally spawned Browncoats above sea level come tied to 2–3 balloons and fly.
- Leader: a naturally spawned Browncoat sometimes (more often at high regional difficulty) holds a Brainz Flag, drops it on death and calls 4–7 more zombies out of the ground: Browncoats, Newspaper, Digger, Disco zombies and All Stars.

## Traits of all mod zombies

The other zombie cards refer to this list.

- They do not burn in daylight and do not turn into Drowned.
- They target plants as well as players, villagers, iron golems and [Garden Gnomes](gnome.md); they never attack each other unless hypnotized.
- The zombies' own special attacks (smashes, missiles, lasers, trampling, their fireworks) do not hurt them unless they come from a hypnotized zombie; plant explosions deal them triple damage.
- After about 12 s with the head under water most of them put on a Ducky Tube and float.
- Zombies tied to balloons fly.
- In a raid they walk to the Plantz Flag and try to break it; raid zombies and summoned reinforcements climb out of the ground.

## Interactions

- Special waves dress it up: buckets and armour (Bucket Brigade, 20 % with an iron Door Shield), balloons (Half-Time Showdown), party hats (Disco of the Undead), pumpkins (Zomboween).
- [Hypno Shroom](hypnoshroom.md) turns it to the player's side; [Grave Buster](grave_buster.md) eats the Gravestones it comes out of.
- A name tag "Tugboat" or "Mustache" gives it a special texture.
