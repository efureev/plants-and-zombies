# Hypno Shroom

**Plant · hypnotist** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A fragile mushroom that turns attackers against their own kind: whoever hits it gets hypnotized.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:hypnoshroom` |
| Health | 4 |
| Sun cost | 7 |
| Size | 0.6 × 1.3 blocks |
| Attack | none; hypnotizes its attacker for 40 s |
| At night | awake; sleeps while it sees the daytime sky |
| How to get | raid rewards in the Mailbox (regular waves, first 8 rewards); dropped by the [Disco Zombie](disco_zombie.md) (4%, when killed by a player or a plant); grows wild in Lush Caves, dark forests, the Warped Forest and mushroom fields; a wild one is tamed with sun (~24% per sun) |

## Abilities

- Zombies that bump into it switch to attacking it, as they do with the [Wall Nut](wallnut.md).
- When an awake Hypno Shroom is hurt by a living attacker, the attacker gets Hypnotized for 40 s.
- If it dies while awake, it leaves a shrinking hypnotic cloud (radius 2.5, 15 s) that hypnotizes anyone inside for about 12 s.
- Hypnotized mobs attack creatures of their own kind; hostile ones attack any other hostile mob. Hypnotized raiders stop heading for the Plantz Flag.
- Asleep, it does nothing: no hypnosis and no cloud.
- Cannot hypnotize plants, the Gargantuar, Electric Slide, Baron Von Bats, robots or bosses.

## Interactions

- Hypnotizing a [Disco Zombie](disco_zombie.md) gives the advancement "Disco is Undead".
- A [Coffee Bean](coffeebean.md) keeps it awake by day for 40 minutes.
- Can wear a [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md); the Pumpkin takes the hits first, but the attacker is still hypnotized.
