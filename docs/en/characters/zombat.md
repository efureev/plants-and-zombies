# Zombat

**Zombie · flyer** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A zombie bat that flies and heals itself with every bite. It haunts the Graveyard, fills Zomboween waves and is the form [Baron Von Bats](baron_von_bats.md) takes as a swarm.

## Abilities

- Always flies: no gravity, no fall damage, silent steps.
- Every bite that lands heals it by 10 % of its maximum health.
- Follow range 24 blocks.
- Cannot hold items, break doors, carry balloons or a Ducky Tube.
- Gets none of the random spawn bonuses of vanilla zombies.
- Shares the [traits of all mod zombies](browncoat.md#traits-of-all-mod-zombies).

## Interactions

- [Baron Von Bats](baron_von_bats.md) turns into 6–7 Zombats for up to 10 s. They split his health, bite for 2 damage and their bites heal him too; the bat that carries him bears his name and passes damage on to him. When the swarm ends, the remaining bats vanish.
