# Royal Sunflower

**Plant · walking laser** · Plants & Zombies (original), experimental

A giant walking sunflower that burns enemies with a long sun beam. It is experimental and hidden unless experimental features are switched on.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:royal_sunflower` |
| Health | 450 |
| Sun cost | none (not in the sun cost list, free to plant) |
| Size | 2.25 × 4.3 blocks |
| Speed | 0.17 (a vanilla zombie: 0.23) |
| Knockback resistance | 0.9 |
| Attack | a sun beam about every 1.5 s, 10 damage to everything along it |
| Range | beam 32 blocks; notices targets within 48 |
| At night | stays awake |
| How to get | creative tab only, after setting `enableExperimentalFeatures` to `true` in `config/sproutsquad/server.json`; no loot, no wild spawns |

## Abilities

- Walks. With a target, it moves to about 8 blocks from it and fires from there.
- The beam is 1.5 blocks wide and passes through every enemy in line; it does energy damage and needs line of sight.
- Does not produce sun.
- Targets zombies (mod and vanilla); a tamed one also hostile mobs; a wild one also players.

## Interactions

- Not required for the "Botanist" advancement (plant one of each plant).
- The other experimental walking plant is the [Citron](citron.md); compare with the ordinary [Sunflower](sunflower.md).
