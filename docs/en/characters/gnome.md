# Garden Gnome

**Other · hostile gnome** · Plants & Zombies (original); its drops were added by Sprout Squad

A small hopping gnome that fights for no side. It attacks players, plants and zombies alike, likes to ride small animals, and sometimes leaves behind a garden gnome statue.

## Abilities

- Targets players, plants and any zombie; other gnomes are left alone unless hypnotized. When hurt, it calls nearby gnomes to join the fight.
- Moves by hopping like a rabbit, climbs full blocks, takes no fall damage and swims fast.
- Weapons: a 33 % chance (50 % on Hard) to spawn with a copper axe, a copper spear, a bow or a copper pickaxe. With a bow it shoots arrows about every 2 s; with a spear it uses the spear charge; otherwise it fights in melee.
- Each armor slot has a 25 % chance of a piece of armor at spawn, drawn at gnome size.
- Picks up items and prefers bows, maces, axes and spears (`sproutsquad:gnome_preferred_weapons`).
- Riding: it looks for an adult animal without a rider within 8 blocks (armadillo, chicken, frog, fox, pig or wolf, tag `sproutsquad:gnome_rideable`), walks up to it and climbs on.

## Variants

- Colour: blue, red, green or yellow is picked at random at spawn. Golden never appears on its own; only a gnome summoned with `color_variant:"golden"` has it.
- Voice: one of two sound sets, picked at random.

## Garden gnome statues

- Blocks `sproutsquad:blue_garden_gnome`, `red_…`, `green_…`, `yellow_…` and `golden_garden_gnome`. Using one cycles its pose: standing, waving, relaxing, sitting, thinking. Statues can be waterlogged and need solid ground below.
- Sources: a gnome's own drop; the bonus hero mail for a raid boss wave (golden is rare there); a 1 % chance of a golden one when a [Marigold](marigold.md) grows seeds.
- The advancement "I'm a Gnome, I'm a Garden Gnome" asks for all five colours.

## Interactions

- Every zombie, vanilla or from the mod, hunts gnomes.
- Player-built machines and the [Lawn Mower](lawn_mower.md) treat gnomes as monsters.
