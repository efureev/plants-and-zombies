# Scaredy Shroom

<p align="center"><img src="../../images/characters/scaredyshroom.png" alt="Scaredy Shroom" width="320"></p>

**Plant · long-range shooter** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A long-range spore shooter that hides as soon as an enemy gets too close.

| | |

| How to get | grows wild in dark forests, taigas, mushroom fields, old-growth forests and taigas, the Dappled Forest and caves; a wild one is tamed with sun (~55% per sun) |

## Abilities

- Hides when a hostile mob, or (for a wild one) a player, comes within 3.5 blocks. It does not shoot while hiding and peeks out once the threat is gone.
- Toxic hurts every 0.75 s; see [Puff Shroom](puffshroom.md).
- Sleeps while the sun is visible to it (daytime and open sky). In caves, under a roof or at night it is awake.
- Targets zombies (mod and vanilla); a tamed one also targets other hostile mobs, except creepers.
- In the wild it also grows on stone, but a seed packet plants it only on soil.

## Interactions

- Fires the same spore as the [Puff Shroom](puffshroom.md), at double damage and more than twice the range.
- A [Coffee Bean](coffeebean.md) keeps it awake by day for 40 minutes.
- Can wear a [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md).
