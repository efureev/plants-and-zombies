# Lightning Reed

**Plant · zapper** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A thin reed that appears in thunderstorms and strikes nearby enemies with electric arcs that jump on to their neighbours.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:lightning_reed` |
| Health | 18 |
| Sun cost | 8 |
| Size | 0.4 × 1.0 blocks |
| Attack | a zap about every 1.3 s, 1.5 damage + Electrified II for 5 s |
| Range | targets within 6.5 blocks; no projectile |
| At night | stays awake |
| Limit | 30 within 32 blocks |
| How to get | grows wild only during thunderstorms, in swamps, mangrove swamps and along rivers (groups of 2–3) |

## Abilities

- Hits the target directly with an arc, so there is no projectile to block or dodge.
- Electrified II: every electric hit on the target, starting with the zap itself, jumps to a random creature of the same side within 3 blocks for half the damage and gives it a weaker Electrified, so the charge runs through a crowd. Electrified creatures standing in water take extra damage.
- Immune to electric damage, lightning included.
- Targets zombies of any kind. A tamed Lightning Reed also targets other monsters, but never creepers; a wild one also zaps players.

## Interactions

- Wild reeds are the only source. Tame one by feeding it sun (about 19% chance per sun), then dig it up with a shovel to get a seed packet, or grow its seeds on farmland.
- Pairs with the [Electric Peashooter](electric_peashooter.md): its electric peas keep spreading the charge from creatures the reed has electrified.
- The [Electric Slide](electric_slide.md) is immune to its electricity.
