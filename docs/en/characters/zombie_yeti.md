# Zombie Yeti

**Zombie · heavy** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A big furry zombie of the snowy lands. It hits very hard, chills its victims and throws chunks of powder snow at targets it cannot reach.

## Abilities

- A melee hit of 13 damage; half of the hits also give Chilled (slower, weaker) for 4 s.
- Snow chunk: when the target is more than 6.5 blocks away and its path cannot reach it, it may throw a chunk of powder snow (25 % chance each time). A direct hit deals 5 damage, then the chunk bursts in a small blast that breaks no blocks; with `mobGriefing` on, the powder snow can stay where it lands.
- While walking to its target it shoves aside players and other non-zombie creatures it bumps into.
- Steps up full blocks; does not put on a Ducky Tube; does not pick up items.
- 6 % spawn with a bucket on the head, 0.5 % riding a polar bear.
- Shares the [traits of all mod zombies](browncoat.md#traits-of-all-mod-zombies).

## Interactions

- Too big for a [Chomper](chomper.md) to swallow.
- Its seed drop is the [Snow Pea](ice_peashooter.md), a plant that slows zombies down.
- Advancement "The Three Zombieteers": defeat an [All Star](all_star.md), a [Disco Zombie](disco_zombie.md) and a Zombie Yeti.
