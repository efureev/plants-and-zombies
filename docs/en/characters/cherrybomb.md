# Cherry Bomb

**Plant · explosive** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A single-use bomb: when an enemy comes close, it swells up and explodes, hitting everything around it.

## Abilities

- Reacts to zombies. A tamed Cherry Bomb also reacts to other monsters; a wild one also to players.
- When a target comes within 3 blocks, it starts to swell and explodes 1.5 s later, disappearing. If the target gets more than 7 blocks away first, the fuse winds back down.
- The explosion does not break blocks and does not harm plants.
- Can be planted on any solid block, not only on soil.

## Interactions

- If it wore pumpkin armor, the [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md) is left in its place after the blast.
- A wider blast: [Explode-O-Nut](explode_o_nut.md); a cheaper trap: [Potatomine](potatomine.md).
- A wild one is tamed by feeding it sun: about 24% chance per sun.
