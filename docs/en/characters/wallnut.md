# Wall Nut

**Plant · wall** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A sturdy living wall: it does not attack, but blocks the way and keeps zombies busy chewing on it.

## Abilities

- Solid like a block: mobs and players cannot walk through it, while items, experience orbs and sun pass through.
- Zombies that bump into it stop and eat it.
- Arrows, tridents, wind charges and firework rockets bounce off it without damage.
- Can be planted on any block and never dries out.
- Always faces the way it was planted. Cracks appear at half health and grow below a quarter.

## Rolling

- Hit your own Wall Nut with a Gardening Glove and it rolls the way you are looking (1 durability; not while it wears pumpkin armor). A piston push also sets it rolling.
- A rolling nut hurts every creature it runs into — the faster it goes, the harder — and knocks it away. Plants are not hurt. The "Strike!" advancement is for hitting 10 mobs with one roll.
- Using the glove on a rolling nut stops it; once it slows down, it settles back onto the block grid.

## Interactions

- Seeds grown on farmland have an 8% chance to become an [Explode-O-Nut](explode_o_nut.md).
- A wild one is tamed by feeding it sun: about 36% chance per sun.
