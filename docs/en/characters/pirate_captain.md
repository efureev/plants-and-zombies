# Pirate Captain

<p align="center"><img src="../../images/characters/pirate_captain.png" alt="Pirate Captain" width="320"></p>

**Zombie · shooter** · Plants & Zombies (original)

The commander of the Pirate Invasion: a sturdy zombie with a crossbow that fires firework rockets. Killing it is only half the job — it comes back as a ghost.

## Abilities

- Fires a firework rocket from its crossbow about every 10 s; the rocket bursts on impact.
- Keeps about 6 blocks from its target and always faces it.
- Its crossbow may be enchanted at spawn, like the weapons of vanilla mobs; it is never dropped.
- On death it turns into a [Pirate Captain Ghost](pirate_captain_ghost.md) that keeps its equipment and stays in the raid. Only damage that bypasses invulnerability (`/kill`, the void) ends it for good.
- Its own loot drops on that first death; it gives no experience (the ghost does).
- Shares the [traits of all mod zombies](browncoat.md#traits-of-all-mod-zombies): its rockets do not hurt zombies.

## Interactions

- During Pirate Invasion it comes with Buccaneer [Browncoats](browncoat.md) and Pirate [Imps](imp.md) carrying powder barrels.
- Advancement "Graveyard Warfare": defeat a [Robo-Zombie](robo_zombie.md), a Pirate Captain and a [Super Brainz](super_brainz.md).
