# Winter Melon

<p align="center"><img src="../../images/characters/winter_melon.png" alt="Winter Melon" width="320"></p>

**Plant · catapult** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A frost version of the [Melon Pult](melonpult.md) that lobs frozen melons and chills everything around the impact. It hits softer and less often than the Melon Pult, but slows the crowd.

## Abilities

- Throws in a high arc, so it reaches targets behind walls, and aims ahead of moving targets.
- The impact pushes back and damages creatures within ~1.25 blocks; the closer they are, the more it hurts.
- Chilled: −10% movement speed and −5% attack damage.
- Targets zombies (mod and vanilla); a tamed one also targets other hostile mobs, except creepers.

## Interactions

- Seed mutation (growing seeds on farmland): a [Melon Pult](melonpult.md) in Snowy Plains, Snowy Taiga, Ice Spikes, Frozen Peaks, Frozen Ocean or Snowy Slopes gives Winter Melon seeds in 1 case of 3.
- A Winter Melon growing seeds outside those biomes gives Melon Pult seeds 15% of the time; inside them it always gives its own.
- Can wear a [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md).
