# Super Brainz

**Zombie · superhero** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A zombie superhero in heavy armour. It punches hard, fires a laser beam that pierces everything in a line and flies up to reach targets above it.

## Abilities

- Punches with alternating fists for 12 damage.
- Laser: standing on the ground, it fires a beam up to 12 blocks long that hits every creature on the line for a quarter of its damage. It keeps firing for about 4 s, moving 30 % slower meanwhile, then waits 14–15 s before the next burst.
- Flight: when its target is more than 3.5 blocks above it, it takes off (checked every 3 s); it lands when it is close to the ground and the target is not above it. No laser while flying.
- Cannot carry balloons; too big for a [Chomper](chomper.md) to swallow; breaks doors.
- Shares the [traits of all mod zombies](browncoat.md#traits-of-all-mod-zombies): its laser does not hurt zombies.

## Interactions

- Electrified: while the effect lasts, electric damage to the target jumps to another creature nearby.
- Advancement "Graveyard Warfare": defeat a [Robo-Zombie](robo_zombie.md), a [Pirate Captain](pirate_captain.md) and a Super Brainz.
