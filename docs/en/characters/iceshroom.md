# Ice Shroom

**Plant · freezer** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A reusable frost bomb: when an enemy comes close, it bursts with cold and freezes everything around it in place.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:iceshroom` |
| Health | 15 |
| Sun cost | 5 |
| Size | 0.6 × 1.0 blocks |
| Attack | frost burst: 2 damage and Frozen for 13 s to everything within 6 blocks; recharges for 24 s |
| Trigger | a target within 4 blocks; fuse ~1.4 s |
| At night | awake; sleeps while it sees the daytime sky |
| How to get | grows wild in the Snowy Taiga, Grove, Ice Spikes, Frozen Peaks and Frozen Ocean (groups of 1–2); a wild one is tamed with sun (~36% per sun) |

## Abilities

- Frozen: the target cannot move, jump, fly or deal damage while the effect lasts.
- The burst also pushes creatures away from it.
- It is not used up: after a burst it recharges and can fire again.
- Plants are never hurt or frozen. A tamed Ice Shroom also spares players and tamed animals (with the default co-op planting); a wild one freezes players too.
- Targets zombies (mod and vanilla); a tamed one also hostile mobs; a wild one also players.
- Can be planted on snow (snow layers, snow blocks, powder snow) as well as on soil.
- Sleeps while the sun is visible to it (daytime and open sky); asleep, it does not go off.

## Interactions

- Uses the same fuse as the [Doom Shroom](doomshroom.md), but freezes instead of exploding and is not used up.
- A [Coffee Bean](coffeebean.md) keeps it awake by day for 40 minutes.
- Can wear a [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md).
