# Sea Shroom

**Plant · aquatic shooter** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A free mushroom that sits on the water and shoots water spores at nearby zombies.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:seashroom` |
| Health | 12 |
| Sun cost | 0 |
| Size | 0.5 × 0.5 blocks |
| Attack | a water spore about every 1.1 s, 0.5 damage |
| Range | 10 blocks |
| At night | awake; sleeps while it sees the daytime sky |
| How to get | fishing (7.5% of fish catches); dropped by the [Pirate Captain Ghost](pirate_captain_ghost.md) (3%, when killed by a player or a plant); grows wild on rivers and deep oceans, in groups of 1–3 (more often in rain); a wild one is always tamed by the first sun |

## Abilities

- Planted on water: use the seed packet on a water surface. On land it survives only on farmland or a Zen Plant Pot.
- Its spores fly through water without slowing down. Unlike the [Puff Shroom](puffshroom.md)'s, they are not toxic.
- Targets zombies (mod and vanilla); a tamed one also targets other hostile mobs, except creepers.
- Breathes under water.
- Sleeps while the sun is visible to it (daytime and open sky).

## Interactions

- Cannot wear a [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md).
- A [Coffee Bean](coffeebean.md) keeps it awake by day for 40 minutes.
- The other plant that is planted on water is the [Tangle Kelp](tanglekelp.md).
