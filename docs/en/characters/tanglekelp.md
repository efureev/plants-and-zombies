# Tangle Kelp

**Plant · aquatic trapper** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A water plant that bites a nearby enemy, roots it in place and drags it towards itself.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:tanglekelp` |
| Health | 25 |
| Sun cost | 2 |
| Size | 1.0 × 0.4 blocks |
| Attack | a bite: 0.75 damage and Tangled for 4 s, then it pulls the target for ~7 s before biting again |
| Range | ~5.75 blocks |
| At night | stays awake |
| How to get | grows wild in the water of swamps and mangrove swamps, in groups of 3–5 (more often in rain); a wild one is tamed with sun (~67% per sun) |

## Abilities

- Planted on water: use the seed packet on a water surface. On land it survives only on farmland or a Zen Plant Pot; anywhere else it dries out.
- Tangled: the target cannot move, jump, swim or attack.
- While the tangle lasts (6.5–8 s) it keeps pulling the target closer, as long as it stays within ~5.75 blocks. The pull pauses while the kelp itself is being hurt.
- A target already caught by another Tangle Kelp is left to that one.
- Anything that bumps into it takes 0.75 damage every second (not plants; for a tamed kelp, not players or its owner's allies).
- Zombies do not pick it as a target.
- Targets zombies; a tamed one also hostile mobs; a wild one also grabs players.
- Breathes under water.

## Interactions

- Cannot wear a [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md).
- The other plant that is planted on water is the [Sea Shroom](seashroom.md).
