# Potatomine

**Plant · mine** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A cheap trap: it arms itself after planting and blows up the first enemy that steps on it.

## Abilities

- After planting it arms for about 8.5–10.5 s and cannot go off during that time.
- Once armed, it explodes as soon as a creature touches it. Plants never set it off, and neither do players if the mine is tamed.
- Single use: it disappears in the explosion. The blast does not break blocks and does not harm plants.
- Zombies and other plant-eaters ignore it and do not attack it, so it waits untouched until someone steps on it.
- Besides soil, it can be planted on sand and gravel.

## Interactions

- Flint and steel lights it while it is idle and awake: it swells up and goes off; the lighter wears out, and someone else's tamed plant follows the usual ownership rules (`coopPlanting`). Sprout Squad fixed this; in the original the lighter never worked.
- It does not go off while a tamed mine is growing seeds on farmland.
- For a stronger blast that does not need contact, see [Cherry Bomb](cherrybomb.md).
