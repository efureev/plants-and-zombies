# Cactus

<p align="center"><img src="../../images/characters/cactus.png" alt="Cactus" width="320"></p>

**Plant · long-range shooter** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A desert sniper: it shoots piercing needles at the farthest enemy it can see and pops balloons.

## Abilities

- Picks the farthest target in range, not the nearest.
- If the target is tied to balloons, it shoots at a balloon first and pops it.
- Needles fly fast and pass through several creatures in a row. A needle stuck in the ground disappears after 5 s.
- Targets zombies of any kind; a tamed Cactus also other monsters, but never creepers. Aims ahead of moving targets and shoots back at whoever hurts it.
- Besides soil, it can be planted on sand, red sand, soul sand and on top of a cactus block.

## Interactions

- Like all plants, it takes no damage from cactus blocks.
- A wild one is tamed by feeding it sun: about 30% chance per sun.
