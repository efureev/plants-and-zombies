# Fire Peashooter

**Plant · shooter** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A Peashooter from the Nether whose peas set targets on fire.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:fire_peashooter` |
| Health | 20 |
| Sun cost | 7 |
| Size | 0.6 × 1.0 blocks |
| Attack | a fire pea about every 1.2 s, 1.25 damage + sets the target on fire for 3.5 s |
| Range | 14 blocks, needs line of sight |
| At night | stays awake |
| Limit | 30 within 32 blocks |
| How to get | grows wild in crimson forests in the Nether (groups of 1–2); [Peashooter](peashooter.md) seeds grown on farmland in a crimson forest turn into it 50% of the time |

## Abilities

- Immune to fire and lava.
- Shoots zombies of any kind; zombified piglins count too. A tamed Fire Peashooter also shoots other monsters, but never creepers; a wild one also shoots players.
- Aims ahead of moving targets and shoots back at whoever hurts it.
- Gives off embers.

## Interactions

- Its seeds grown in a crimson forest always stay Fire Peashooter; elsewhere they have a 15% chance to revert to a plain [Peashooter](peashooter.md).
- An ordinary pea from a [Peashooter](peashooter.md) or [Repeater](repeater.md) that flies through fire also becomes a fire pea.
- A wild one is tamed by feeding it sun: about 24% chance per sun.
- Named `blaze` with a name tag, it gets a different look.
