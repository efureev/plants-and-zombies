# Melon Pult

**Plant · catapult** · Plants & Zombies (original)

The heaviest catapult: slow, but every melon hits hard and damages everything around the impact.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:melonpult` |
| Health | 35 |
| Sun cost | 10 |
| Size | 0.9 × 0.8 blocks |
| Attack | a melon about every 3.9 s, 4.5 damage + splash within 1.5 blocks |
| Range | 38 blocks, no line of sight needed |
| At night | stays awake |
| Limit | 20 within 32 blocks |
| How to get | grows wild in jungles (rare, alone) |

## Abilities

- Splash: creatures within about 1.5 blocks of the impact also take damage — the closer, the more — and are knocked back. Plants are not affected.
- Throws in a high arc, so it hits enemies behind walls and fences that would block a straight shot. Aims ahead of moving targets.
- Targets zombies of any kind; a tamed Melon Pult also other monsters, but never creepers. Fights back against whoever hurts it.
- Its planting limit is 20 instead of the usual 30.

## Interactions

- Seeds grown on farmland in snowy biomes have a 33% chance to become a [Winter Melon](winter_melon.md).
- Lighter catapults: [Cabbage Pult](cabbagepult.md), [Kernel Pult](kernelpult.md).
- A wild one is tamed by feeding it sun: about 12% chance per sun.
