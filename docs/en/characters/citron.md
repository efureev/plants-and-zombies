# Citron

**Plant · walking laser** · Plants & Zombies (original), experimental

A walking citrus that chases enemies and shoots a thin laser beam at them. It is experimental and hidden unless experimental features are switched on.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:citron` |
| Health | 50 |
| Sun cost | none (not in the sun cost list, free to plant) |
| Size | 0.85 × 0.76 blocks |
| Speed | 0.22 (a vanilla zombie: 0.23) |
| Knockback resistance | 0.1 |
| Attack | a laser about every 1.65 s, 3 damage to everything along it |
| Range | 32 blocks |
| At night | stays awake |
| How to get | creative tab only, after setting `enableExperimentalFeatures` to `true` in `config/sproutsquad/server.json`; no loot, no wild spawns |

## Abilities

- Walks towards its target and keeps shooting on the move.
- The laser is 1.5 blocks wide and passes through every enemy in line; it does energy damage and needs line of sight.
- A player who hits a tamed Citron with a Gardening Glove sends it rolling like a [Wall Nut](wallnut.md); it bounces along until it slows down, and each hit wears the glove.
- Targets zombies (mod and vanilla); a tamed one also hostile mobs; a wild one also players.

## Interactions

- Not required for the "Botanist" advancement (plant one of each plant).
- The other experimental walking plant is the [Royal Sunflower](royal_sunflower.md).
