# Pirate Captain Ghost

**Zombie · ghost** · Plants & Zombies (original)

The second life of the [Pirate Captain](pirate_captain.md): a flying ghost with a sword that passes through walls.

## Abilities

- Always flies and passes through blocks; it always sees its target, even through walls.
- Fights in melee with an iron sword that it never drops.
- Follow range 100 blocks: it notices and chases targets from very far.
- Silent steps and death; cannot carry balloons or a Ducky Tube; does not pick up items.
- Gives 90 experience.
- Shares the [traits of all mod zombies](browncoat.md#traits-of-all-mod-zombies).

## Interactions

- A ghost made from a raid's captain stays in that raid; walls and moats do not keep it out.
