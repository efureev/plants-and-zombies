# Repeater

**Plant · shooter** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A Peashooter that fires peas in pairs, doing twice the damage of the ordinary one.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:repeater` |
| Health | 20 |
| Sun cost | 7 |
| Size | 0.6 × 1.0 blocks |
| Attack | two peas about every 1 s, 1 damage each |
| Range | 14 blocks, needs line of sight |
| At night | stays awake |
| Limit | 30 within 32 blocks |
| How to get | [Peashooter](peashooter.md) seeds grown on farmland turn into it 10% of the time; raid rewards from the Mailbox; does not grow in the wild |

## Abilities

- Fires a pea, a second one about 0.35 s later, then pauses.
- Picks targets like the [Peashooter](peashooter.md): zombies of any kind; a tamed Repeater also other monsters, but never creepers. Aims ahead of moving targets.
- Shoots back at whoever hurts it; a tamed one also attacks what its owner attacks and whatever attacks its owner.
- A pea that flies through fire turns into a fire pea and sets the target alight.

## Interactions

- Unlike the [Peashooter](peashooter.md), it is not changed by lightning.
- Other pea plants: [Snow Pea](ice_peashooter.md), [Fire Peashooter](fire_peashooter.md), [Electric Peashooter](electric_peashooter.md).
