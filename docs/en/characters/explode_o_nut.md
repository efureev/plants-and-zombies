# Explode-O-Nut

**Plant · explosive wall** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A Wall Nut with a surprise: it holds the line like an ordinary one, but explodes when an enemy finishes it off.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:explode_o_nut` |
| Health | 50 |
| Armor | 8 (toughness 5) |
| Sun cost | 5 |
| Size | 1.0 × 1.13 blocks |
| Attack | an explosion with radius 4 when it dies from a hit or rolls into a creature |
| At night | stays awake |
| Limit | 30 within 32 blocks |
| How to get | only by seed mutation: [Wall Nut](wallnut.md) seeds grown on farmland turn into it 8% of the time; does not grow in the wild |

## Abilities

- Does everything a [Wall Nut](wallnut.md) does: it is solid, arrows bounce off it, zombies stop to eat it, it can be planted on any block and rolls when hit with the Gardening Glove.
- Explodes and disappears when:
  - it is killed by a direct attack — a zombie bite, a melee hit or a projectile;
  - while rolling, it runs into a creature that is not a plant;
  - while rolling, it falls more than 12 blocks.
- The explosion does not break blocks and does not harm plants.
- If it wore pumpkin armor, the [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md) is left in its place after the blast.

## Interactions

- Its seeds grown on farmland have a 20% chance to come out as a plain [Wall Nut](wallnut.md).
- Its blast is wider than the [Cherry Bomb](cherrybomb.md)'s (radius 4 against 2.5).
