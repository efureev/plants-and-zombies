# Fume Shroom

**Plant · fume sprayer** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A mushroom that sprays toxic fumes through every enemy in a line in front of it.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:fumeshroom` |
| Health | 20 |
| Sun cost | 6 |
| Size | 0.8 × 0.8 blocks |
| Attack | fumes about every 2.35 s: 2 damage and Toxic II for 5 s to every target in the line |
| Range | 8 blocks, a line 2 blocks wide |
| At night | awake; sleeps while it sees the daytime sky |
| How to get | chests in the Graveyard mausoleum; grows wild in dark forests, taigas, mushroom fields, old-growth forests and taigas, the Dappled Forest and caves; a wild one is tamed with sun (~30% per sun) |

## Abilities

- The spray goes through all enemies along the line, so it hurts a whole column of zombies at once. It needs line of sight to its target.
- Toxic II hurts twice as often as normal Toxic (every ~0.35 s) and twice as hard; see [Puff Shroom](puffshroom.md).
- Sleeps while the sun is visible to it (daytime and open sky). In caves, under a roof or at night it is awake.
- Targets zombies (mod and vanilla); a tamed one also targets other hostile mobs, creepers included.
- In the wild it also grows on stone, but a seed packet plants it only on soil.

## Interactions

- A [Coffee Bean](coffeebean.md) keeps it awake by day for 40 minutes.
- Can wear a [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md).
