# Digger Zombie

**Zombie · tunneller** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A miner with an iron pickaxe that lives deep underground. From a distance it burrows into the ground and pops up close to its target, skipping walls and defences.

## Abilities

- Tunnelling: when its target (in a raid without a target: the Plantz Flag) is 16–40 blocks away, it stops, sinks into the ground and after about 2 s resurfaces 78–93 % of the way there, on solid ground with room to stand. The whole move takes about 4 s.
- After a tunnel (and after spawning) it waits 16–20 s before the next one; it does not tunnel while in water.
- Carries an iron pickaxe that it never drops; does not pick up armour; breaks doors.
- Shares the [traits of all mod zombies](browncoat.md#traits-of-all-mod-zombies).

## Interactions

- Walls between it and its target do not stop the tunnel: the exit point is chosen near the target, not along a path.
- Leader groups and Gravestones can also bring Digger Zombies to the surface.
