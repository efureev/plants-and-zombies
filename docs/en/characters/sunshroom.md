# Sun Shroom

<p align="center"><img src="../../images/characters/sunshroom.png" alt="Sun Shroom" width="320"></p>

**Plant · sun producer** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A sun maker for the dark: it produces sun at night, in caves and under a roof, and gives more once it has grown up.

## Abilities

- Does not need sunlight: it produces sun whenever it is awake.
- Sleeps, and produces nothing, while the sun is visible to it (daytime and open sky).
- Starts small after planting and grows up after ~3,400 ticks (about 2.8 min).
- Does not attack and does not fight back.

## Interactions

- The [Sunflower](sunflower.md) gives 1 sun about every 45 s, but only in sunlight; a grown Sun Shroom gives more sun per minute, but only out of the sun.
- A [Coffee Bean](coffeebean.md) keeps it awake, and producing, by day for 40 minutes.
- Can wear a [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md).
