# Backup Dancer

<p align="center"><img src="../../images/characters/backup_dancer.png" alt="Backup Dancer" width="320"></p>

**Zombie · minion** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A fast, fragile dancer that the [Disco Zombie](disco_zombie.md) summons in groups of up to three.

## Abilities

- Climbs out of the ground next to its Disco Zombie and goes for the target in melee.
- Faster than a [Browncoat](browncoat.md) (0.285 against 0.23), but has little health; follow range 24 blocks.
- Gets none of the random spawn bonuses of vanilla zombies; cannot break doors.
- Summoned dancers ignore the raid flag.
- Shares the [traits of all mod zombies](browncoat.md#traits-of-all-mod-zombies).

## Interactions

- If it deals the killing blow to a [Disco Zombie](disco_zombie.md), the Disco Zombie drops the music disc "Grassy Groove"; a dancer hypnotized by a [Hypno Shroom](hypnoshroom.md) can do this.
- The Party Hat it drops is a head item with 1 armour.
