# Electric Slide

<p align="center"><img src="../../images/characters/electric_slide.png" alt="Electric Slide" width="320"></p>

**Zombie · electric** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A disco zombie on roller skates that zaps its targets with electricity. It is fast, armoured and immune to electric damage.

## Abilities

- Electric punch: half its attack damage as electric damage, about once a second, at close range.
- Zap: it fires an electric arc up to 12 blocks long that hits every creature on the line for a quarter of its damage and makes them Electrified for 5 s. It keeps zapping for about 4 s, then waits 14–15 s before the next burst.
- Keeps about 2.5 blocks from its target.
- Immune to electric damage: lightning, the [Electric Peashooter](electric_peashooter.md), the [Lightning Reed](lightning_reed.md), Electrified chains.
- Cannot be hypnotized; too big for a [Chomper](chomper.md) to swallow (over 100 health).
- Wears unbreakable Roller Skates that it never drops from the slot.
- Gives 90 experience.
- Shares the [traits of all mod zombies](browncoat.md#traits-of-all-mod-zombies).

## Interactions

- Electrified: while the effect lasts, electric damage to the target jumps to another creature nearby.
- As a raid boss it takes only 40 damage from a player's [Lawn Mower](lawn_mower.md) instead of being mowed down (Sprout Squad).
- Advancement "Head Honchos": defeat a [Baron Von Bats](baron_von_bats.md), an Electric Boogaloo and a [Gargantuar](gargantuar.md).
