# Puff Shroom

**Plant · shooter** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A free short-range mushroom that shoots toxic spores; it works at night and sleeps in daylight.

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | `sproutsquad:puffshroom` |
| Health | 12 |
| Sun cost | 0 |
| Size | 0.5 × 0.65 blocks |
| Attack | a spore about every 1.1 s: 0.5 damage and Toxic for 2.5 s |
| Range | 10 blocks |
| At night | awake; sleeps while it sees the daytime sky |
| How to get | grows wild in dark forests, taigas, mushroom fields, old-growth forests and taigas, the Dappled Forest and caves (groups of 1–4); a wild one is always tamed by the first sun |

## Abilities

- Toxic hurts every 0.75 s for 4 × the target's remaining share of health (at least 0.4 damage), so it hits healthy targets hardest.
- Sleeps while the sun is visible to it (daytime and open sky). In caves, under a roof or at night it is awake.
- Targets zombies (mod and vanilla); a tamed one also targets other hostile mobs, except creepers.
- In the wild it also grows on stone, but a seed packet plants it only on soil (grass, dirt, moss and the like).

## Interactions

- A [Coffee Bean](coffeebean.md) keeps it awake by day for 40 minutes.
- The [Scaredy Shroom](scaredyshroom.md) fires the same spore at double damage and more than twice the range.
- The [Sea Shroom](seashroom.md) has the same stats on water, but its spores are not toxic.
- Can wear a [Pumpkin](pumpkin.md).
