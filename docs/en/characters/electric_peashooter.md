# Electric Peashooter

<p align="center"><img src="../../images/characters/electric_peashooter.png" alt="Electric Peashooter" width="320"></p>

**Plant · shooter** · Plants & Zombies (original)

A Peashooter charged by lightning: its peas electrify enemies, and the charge jumps from one to another.

## Abilities

- Electrified: while it lasts, every further electric hit on the target arcs to one random creature of the same side within 3 blocks (monster to monster) for half the damage. An electrified creature standing in water takes 1.25 damage every 0.6 s.
- Immune to electric damage, lightning included.
- Shoots zombies of any kind. A tamed Electric Peashooter also shoots other monsters, but never creepers; a wild one also shoots players.
- Aims ahead of moving targets and shoots back at whoever hurts it.

## Interactions

- Its seeds grown during a thunderstorm always stay Electric; otherwise they have a 15% chance to revert to a plain [Peashooter](peashooter.md).
- Combines with the [Lightning Reed](lightning_reed.md): a creature electrified by the reed passes the charge, together with a weaker Electrified, to a neighbour every time an electric pea hits it.
- The [Electric Slide](electric_slide.md) is immune to its peas' electricity.
